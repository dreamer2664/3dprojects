## E. Honest limitations of this reference work

1. **No image gallery in this list was inspected image-by-image.** The environment has no browser;
   `fetch_page` returns text, not images. Every visual decision in this build therefore rests on
   textual descriptions (which for the Yola Gray desks are unusually detailed: drawer counts,
   compartment counts, lining species, knob material, lock behaviour) plus construction literature.
   Where the texts conflict (lacewood vs sycamore linings; 8 vs 27 vs 33 internal drawers) the log
   records both and the build states which it followed and why.
2. **Two sources (#36, #37) self-identify as AI-generated wood identification.** They are logged
   for completeness and corroboration only, and were not used as authority for any decision.
3. A further 4 URLs were opened by the search tool but contained no usable content for this asset
   (Etsy and Amazon hardware listing pages, and an Amazon product page whose images did not load);
   they are not counted in the 45 above.
4. The brief asked for "15 or more photographs … including open and closed states and as many rear
   / side views as possible". Photographs could not be retrieved, so that specific sub-requirement
   is **not** met in the sense intended (visual study of photographs). The source mix above is met
   by page type, not by image count. This is stated again in `QUALITY_GATE.md`.
