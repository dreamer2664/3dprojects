# DECISIONS & ASSUMPTIONS

## D-001 Tooling (2026-11-18 20:30 UTC)
- **Blender 4.2.3 LTS** (official linux-x64 build), headless. The whole model is generated **procedurally by Python scripts in `scripts/`** and saved to `MODELS/*.blend`. Reason: reproducibility, diffable history in git, and the sandbox has no GUI. The `.blend` is fully editable (real meshes, modifiers kept live where useful).
- Sandbox limits: 2 vCPU, ~1 GB RAM, no GPU → Cycles CPU with denoising (OpenImageDenoise), moderate samples, 1600–1920 px renders. Scene complexity is managed with modifiers applied only at render time.

## D-002 Coordinate system & units
- Metres, Z up. **+X = forward**, **+Y = rider's left**, origin = ground point directly below the **rear axle**.
- Left orthographic = camera on +Y looking −Y. Right = camera on −Y.

## D-004 Engine internals
- External castings modelled to match photographs and exploded diagrams. Internals (crank, rods, pistons, valves, cams, gearbox shafts) modelled **plausibly to scale** (81 mm pistons, 53.32 mm stroke → 26.66 mm crank throw, 65° bank angle) for the exploded and cut-away renders. Exact internal shapes (rod length, cam profiles, gear tooth counts other than published ratios) are **not published** → assumed: rod centre-to-centre 106 mm (typical superbike ratio ≈2.0×stroke), valve heads Ø33 mm intake / Ø27 mm exhaust (assumed, typical 81 mm-bore 4V).
- Bank orientation: front bank axis 37° above horizontal (forward), rear bank 37°+65° = 102° → ASSUMPTION from side photographs.

## D-005 Livery
- The graphics are reproduced as procedural/painted textures generated by script (`scripts/make_textures.py`) following the photographs. The **"aprilia" and "RSV4" logotypes** are reproduced as simple vector text approximations only where the reference shows them; no invented lettering elsewhere. Tyre sidewall lettering is omitted (not legible in references) rather than invented.

## D-006 Frame colour
- 2026 Shakedown Indigo Factory has a **black** frame (verified in p_17/p_18 and official gallery) — unlike the silver frame in the 2019–20 workshop manual photos. Swingarm natural satin aluminium.
